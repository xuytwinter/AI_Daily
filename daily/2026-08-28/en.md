2. Google Launches New Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash Video Model with 4K Resolution Output

Google has launched a new Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash video generation AI model, achieving breakthroughs in high-definition and long-duration video generation in the multi-modal field. Its features include scene expansion, camera movement control, and the introduction of reference video clips, enhancing the flexibility and professionalism of video generation. At the same time, the model offers multiple resolution options and adopts a tiered pricing strategy, lowering the entry barrier.

![image.png](https://upload.chinaz.com/2026/0828/6392350503400244293580753.png)

> AiBase Summary:
> 🎥 Scene expansion allows users to seamlessly continue generating subsequent frames at the end of an existing video, with each expansion step lasting 10 seconds, allowing up to 40 seconds of continuous video.
> 📽 Camera movement control enables smooth transitions and professional shots by specifying the start and end frames of a shot.
> 📹 The new model supports introducing reference video clips up to 3 seconds long, ensuring accurate contextual consistency and character continuity when building new scenes.
