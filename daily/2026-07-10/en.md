5. Ant Lingbo opensources LingBot-Video, the world's first video foundation model for embodied intelligence!

The model performed well in the RBench benchmark test, demonstrating strong physical understanding and action consistency. Its innovative architecture and training method make it more suitable for robot operations and real-time interaction needs.

![image.png](https://upload.chinaz.com/2026/0709/6391919221763872223135142.png)

> 【AiBase Summary:】
> 🧠 LingBot-Video is the world's first video generation foundation model based on MoE architecture, specifically designed for embodied intelligence.
> 🧪 In the RBench benchmark test, LingBot-Video outperformed multiple mainstream models, showing stronger physical understanding and action consistency.
> 🚀 Through DiT + MoE architecture, embodied data, and a multidimensional reinforcement learning reward system, LingBot-Video achieves efficient reasoning combined with real physical laws.
