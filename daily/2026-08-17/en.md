1. Off-peak pricing, peak pricing doubles: DeepSeek API peak-valley pricing launched, V4Pro up to 11 times higher

DeepSeek has officially launched its new peak-valley time-based pricing for DeepSeek API, using price levers to guide developers to schedule tasks during off-peak hours, alleviating daytime computing power congestion. The cost of API calls during idle periods is only half that of peak hours, and the prices of two main models are also effective. The price increase of V4Pro during peak hours is significant, but it still has an advantage compared to the Claude flagship model. This adjustment marks a new stage in DeepSeek's refined computing power operations.

> AiBase Summary:
> 📅 DeepSeek has launched new peak-valley time-based pricing for DeepSeek API, with prices during idle periods being only half of those during peak hours.
> 💰 The peak hour price of DeepSeek V4Pro increased by 1100%, and the output price increased by 350%.
> 📊 The peak-valley pricing mechanism marks DeepSeek's departure from the "extreme cost-effectiveness" strategy, offering a new model for refined computing power operations.
