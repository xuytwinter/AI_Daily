1. OpenAI releases GPT-5.6Sol, triggering security warnings, new flagship model exposed to autonomously deleting user databases

3. Douyin Qianwen and Tongyi Qianwen simultaneously discontinue intelligent agent functions, national first AI human-like interactive service regulation comes into effect today

The article states that Douyin and Tongyi Qianwen have officially discontinued their intelligent agent functions due to regulatory requirements. The new regulation, issued jointly by five departments, aims to regulate AI human-like interaction services, focusing on AI products with personality settings and emotional companionship, such as virtual partners. At the same time, safety assessments and algorithm filings have become industry standards, marking the beginning of a standardized phase in China's AI management.
