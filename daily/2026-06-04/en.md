2. Understand First, Then Act! ByteDance Opens Bernini Framework, Making AI Video Editing No Longer a Guess

The article introduces ByteDance's open-source Bernini framework, which solves issues like uncontrolled visuals and flickering frames in traditional video generation and editing through a "first understand, then generate" collaborative mechanism. Bernini splits the workflow into semantic planning and visual rendering, improving the practical value of controllable editing and supporting images and videos as visual references, greatly enhancing creative consistency.

![image.png](https://upload.chinaz.com/2026/0603/6391609087007949638700332.png)

> AiBase Summary:
> 🧠 A multi-modal large model planner deeply analyzes input materials to generate semantic sketches.
> 🎨 A renderer based on Diffusion Transformer performs high-quality visual rendering.
> 🔄 Introduces SA-3D RoPE positional encoding mechanism to solve challenges in connecting multiple visual segments.
