2. AutoNet launches the world's first 3D native city world model ABot-Earth0.5, now open for internal testing

The global first 3D native city world model ABot-Earth0.5 launched by AutoNet Group marks a major breakthrough in spatial intelligence technology in the field of city-level 3D reconstruction. This model covers 190 countries and regions around the world and has highly automated characteristics, greatly improving the production efficiency of 3D spatial data. The 3DGS format materials output by it can be seamlessly imported into mainstream games and real-time rendering engines, providing high-precision 3D geographic bases for autonomous driving, low-altitude route planning, and digital twin cities. Currently, the model is officially open for internal testing and plans to continue iteration to promote the industrialization of spatial generative AI.

![image.png](https://upload.chinaz.com/2026/0608/6391652416418503746162779.jpg)

> AiBase Summary:
> 🌍 The world's first 3D native city world model ABot-Earth0.5 is released, marking a major breakthrough in spatial intelligence technology.

> 🚀 The 3DGS format materials output by ABot-Earth0.5 can be seamlessly imported into mainstream game and real-time rendering engines for interactive development.
