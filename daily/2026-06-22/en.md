4. Xiaomi Opensource Full-House Smart AI Solution Miloco 2.0: Let the Home Remember, Recognize People, and Understand Execution

Xiaomi officially released and open-sourced its future-oriented full-house smart AI open-source solution — Xiaomi Miloco 2.0. Based on the self-developed MiMo large model, it is integrated into the OpenClaw framework in the form of an Agent, achieving comprehensive upgrades in interaction methods, product functions, and memory systems. Miloco 2.0 brings four core capabilities: general common sense, identity recognition, family memory, and family tasks, enabling the system to have proactive intelligence, able to complete tasks before users even speak. It requires at least 4GB of memory and 256GB of storage, recommends using Mac mini for continuous operation, and supports macOS and Linux systems.

![image.png](https://pic.chinaz.com/picmap/201811151725243067_1.jpg)

> 【AiBase Summary:】
> 🧠 General common sense capability allows the system to automatically identify potential dangers and issue graded warnings.
> 👥 Identity recognition function can integrate facial and body information, actively register new members.
> 💡 Family memory function records family habits and preferences, supports autonomous execution in complex scenarios.
