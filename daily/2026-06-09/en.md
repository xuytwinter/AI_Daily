1. No "shell game"! Apple responds directly to the iOS 27 AI controversy: Apple Foundation Models are fully self-developed

After the release of iOS 27, Apple made a clear response to external doubts about whether its AI system, Apple Foundation Models (AFM), was based on Google Gemini. Apple emphasized that AFM is a completely self-developed technical system, with core architecture, code logic, and data systems built by the Apple team. It also demonstrated the hierarchical architecture of AFM and its edge-cloud collaboration capabilities.

![image.png](https://pic.chinaz.com/picmap/202206071118455737_0.jpg)

> AiBase Summary:
> 🍎 Apple Foundation Models is an AI system independently developed by Apple, with no direct connection to Google Gemini.
> ⚙️ AFM is divided into edge models and cloud models, handling local and high-load tasks respectively, ensuring performance optimization.
> 🔒 Apple maintains privacy and security standards in cloud deployment, ensuring user data is not affected by third parties.
