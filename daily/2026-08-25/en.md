4. Grok Build Gets Its Strongest Add-on! Integrates Browser-Use Plugin, AI Officially Takes Over Your Browser

5. Chinese Open Source Models Go Global: Legal AI Unicorn Harvey Launches Tenet Based on Kimi K3

7. ARIA Cracks Down on Pure AI Songs: Human-Created Vocals Can Be Listed, AI-Assisted Still Allowed

The Australian Recording Industry Association (ARIA) has decided to ban songs entirely generated by AI from appearing on official charts, with the new rule taking effect next week. This move aims to ensure human dominance in music creation and respond to controversies over the use of unauthorized works to train AI tools on streaming platforms.

![image.png](https://pic.chinaz.com/picmap/202305251639285414_2.jpg)

> 【AiBase Summary:】
> 🎧 ARIA bans songs entirely generated by AI from appearing on official charts.
> 🎤 Songs must be created, sung, and played primarily by humans to be listed.
> 🤖 Partially AI-assisted works are still allowed, but must use legitimate AI services.
