1. Meituan officially released the trillion-parameter open-source large model LongCat-2.0, which supports 1M ultra-long context natively

Meituan officially released and open-sourced the new generation of trillion-parameter large model LongCat-2.0. The model was trained and inferred on a domestic computing cluster, with a total of 1.6T parameters and native support for 1M ultra-long context. Its preview version ranked among the top three globally in monthly usage on the OpenRouter platform and performed outstandingly in multiple ecosystems. The release of LongCat-2.0 marks a major breakthrough in large-scale cluster training using domestic computing power. Its architecture is designed around real Agentic Coding tasks, introducing sparse attention mechanisms and zero-computation expert mechanisms, making the model perform exceptionally well in complex scenarios such as code understanding, mathematical reasoning, and long-range retrieval, even surpassing GPT-5.5 and Claude Opus4.6.
