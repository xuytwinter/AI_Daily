1. Tongyi Lab jointly opens source the first unified scientific large model LOGOS, which outperforms NatureLM with 1B parameters

The model achieves cross-domain knowledge integration and autoregressive generation through innovative scientific syntax design and spatial interaction discretization technology. 2. NetEase Cloud Music's AI Emotional Companion App "Miaoshi" Announces Shutdown on July 14th

NetEase Cloud Music's AI emotional companion app "Miaoshi" announced that it will shut down completely on July 14th. This business adjustment is part of normal development. The article points out that the current AI virtual partner industry faces strong regulation, with the National Cybersecurity Administration and five other departments issuing relevant management measures to combat content involving pornography and violence. At the same time, many apps have compliance loopholes, leading to an overhaul. This shutdown reflects the industry's transition from unregulated growth to compliance and order, making compliance a core issue for all practitioners.

![image.png](https://pic.chinaz.com/picmap/201811151633428242_17.jpg)

> 【AiBase Summary:】
> 🎮 AI virtual partner apps face strict regulation, with the National Cybersecurity Administration and five other departments issuing the "Interim Measures for the Management of Artificial Intelligence Personification Interaction Services."
> 📉 "Miaoshi" shutdown reflects a deep shakeout in the AI emotional companion sector, with compliance becoming the core issue.
> 🔄 Users can apply for refunds and export data; the platform has stopped new user registration and recharge services.

5. From "Drawing Cats" to "Seeing Through the Body": Midjourney Enters the Medical Field, Launches a Full-Body Ultrasound Scanner

Midjourney expands into the medical field, launching its first hardware product—a full-body scanner based on ultrasound technology, aiming to extend AI computing power from image generation to quantitative perception of human anatomy. The device uses a ring sensor array and ultrasound technology to generate high-precision 3D slice images quickly, offering users a convenient way to manage their health. However, its application in the medical field still faces regulatory challenges, and it currently focuses on non-clinical diagnostic body composition maps.

![image.png](https://pic.chinaz.com/picmap/202305050929563629_6.jpg)

> 📊 CEO David Holz envisions future users scanning frequently to quantify and track body changes, but currently, the focus is on non-clinical diagnostic body composition maps.
