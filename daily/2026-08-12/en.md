3. LTX officially released the open-world model LTX-2.5: Native integration with ComfyUI and free for small and medium-sized enterprises

LTX officially released the open-world model LTX-2.5, which has undergone deep restructuring of the generation pipeline, including the introduction of a new diffusion video decoder, support for native multi-camera single-pass rendering generation, and the deployment of a custom Gemma4 language backbone network. At the same time, LTX adopts a tiered licensing strategy, allowing small and medium-sized enterprises to use it for free, while large enterprises need to negotiate authorization. The model is now available on Hugging Face and has formed a strategic partnership with ComfyUI for native integration.

![image.png](https://upload.chinaz.com/2026/0812/6392214384838468134079183.jpg)

> 【AiBase Summary:】
> 🎥 Introduces a new diffusion video decoder to enhance details and image quality
> 🔄 Supports native multi-camera single-pass rendering generation
> ⚙️ Equipped with a custom Gemma4 language backbone network and dedicated pre-training checkpoints
