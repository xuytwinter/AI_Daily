1. MiniMax launches Music3 music model: Generate a 5-minute song with lyrics and descriptions

MiniMax-Music3 is a music generation model that can generate a complete 5-minute song based on lyrics and music descriptions. It uses a hierarchical autoregressive architecture, where a global language model and a local language model work together, responsible for long-range semantics and structural changes of the song, as well as fine-grained acoustic information completion. The model can maintain the musical theme, rhythm, vocal identity, and arrangement progress in long audio, and supports various music structures such as prelude, verse, and chorus.

![image.png](https://pic.chinaz.com/2026/0814/26081404220591834270.jpg)

> AiBase Summary:
> 🎵 The global language model is responsible for the long-range semantics and structural changes of the song.
> 🔊 The local language model is responsible for predicting the acoustic codebook of each frame and supplementing fine-grained acoustic information.
> 🎼 The model can generate a 5-minute song containing multiple music structures.
> More details: https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-Music3
