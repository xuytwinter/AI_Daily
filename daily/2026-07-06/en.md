2. Doubao and Qwen Both Remove AI Anthropomorphic Features: New Regulations Take Effect on July 15, Industry Goes into Contraction

Two mainstream AI applications in China, Doubao and Qwen, will remove customizable anthropomorphic agent features to adapt to new regulations. The new regulations clearly regulate services that simulate human personality traits, thinking patterns, and communication methods, and provide continuous emotional interaction, but exclude tools such as customer service robots. Users express dissatisfaction with the removal of AI anthropomorphic features, believing these features are their emotional support, and the lack of data migration paths causes a sense of fragmentation.

5. Meta Adjusts AI Development Pace: Llama API Public Preview Will Be Discontinued Tomorrow
