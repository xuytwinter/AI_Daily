1. Support whispering and emotion control! Fish Audio launches S2.1Pro real-time dialogue voice model for its anniversary

Fish Audio launched its latest production-level voice large model S2.1Pro on the occasion of its anniversary. The model is specifically designed for real-time voice conversation scenarios, featuring extremely low latency and high-fidelity interactive capabilities, while supporting multi-speaker dialogue generation and high-quality voice cloning.

![image.png](https://upload.chinaz.com/2026/0729/6392093257857185022660369.jpg)

> 【AiBase Summary:】
> 🗣️ S2.1Pro achieves a first-frame audio playback delay of about 90 milliseconds and supports natural and smooth conversation alternation.
> 🌐 The model covers 83 languages and uses a unified speech recognition architecture.
> 🎙️ Supports whispering and emotion control, allowing free-form bracket tags to be embedded directly in the text.

5. AI Services on Xianyu Sell Nearly 10 Million Orders in Half a Year: Sellers in Fourth-Tier Cities Are Most, Women Account for Sixty Percent

Data from the first half of 2026 shows that AI services became the most popular consumer category on the Xianyu platform. The order volume reached 9.816 million, an increase of 157% year-on-year, with nearly 5 million users purchasing AI services, an increase of 98% year-on-year. AI skill orders were the largest category, accounting for 45.1% of all AI orders. AI programming and website building orders increased by 1732%, and AI manga orders increased by 1425%. The seller profile showed that people aged 18 to 35 accounted for more than 60%, and women sellers accounted for 62.4%. Sellers in fourth-tier cities had the highest proportion, at 32.2%.
