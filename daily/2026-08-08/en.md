3、Volc Engine Launches Seedance 2.5 API, Video Generation Capabilities Fully Upgraded

Volc Engine officially launched the Seedance 2.5 API service. In the new version, improvements were made in instruction following, long narratives, realism, audio-visual quality, and other aspects, further enhancing AI video generation capabilities. Seedance 2.5 supports 30-second video output natively, up to 50 multimodal material references, and offers more accurate and stable video editing capabilities, while being compatible with more than ten languages. The model further optimizes key video elements such as image quality, audio quality, lighting, camera movement, and overall aesthetics, making AI-generated content closer to movie-level long narrative effects. Enterprise practice shows that the realism and visual quality of video generation are important foundations for expanding commercial applications. The upgrade of Seedance 2.5 will provide more possibilities for creative advertising and industrial simulation scenarios. Currently, Seedance 2.5 has been simultaneously launched on multiple platforms, providing enterprises and creators with more AI video production tools. In addition, Seedance 2.0 mini and Seedance 2.0 fast have opened limited-time promotions, further lowering the threshold for AI video creation and accelerating the application of generative video technology in the business field.

7、Google Releases Offline Translation Hardware Gemma Translator: Raspberry Pi with 5.1 Billion Parameters, Full Offline Cross-Language Communication

Google released a new offline translation device called Gemma Translator, which is based on its own Gemma4E2B model, featuring 5.1 billion total parameters and 2.3 billion activated parameters, specifically designed for resource-constrained edge devices such as mobile phones, browsers, and Raspberry Pi. The device runs on the Raspberry Pi 5 single-board computer, enabling speech transcription, translation, and speech synthesis, all without needing to be online, ensuring cross-language communication capabilities even when offline.
