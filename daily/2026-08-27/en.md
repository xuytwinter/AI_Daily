4. Copying AI Software to Start a Business, Beijing's First AIGC Commercial Secret Case Fined 100,000 Yuan

The Chaoyang District Market Supervision Bureau of Beijing made a decision on the first administrative penalty case in the AIGC industry involving commercial secrets, issuing a fine of 100,000 yuan for an employee who privately copied and used the original unit's AI commercial software, marking a substantive step in Beijing's commercial secret protection in the new digital economy model.

![image.png](https://pic.chinaz.com/picmap/202304121052180076_0.jpg)

> AiBase Highlights:
> 💻 An employee secretly copied and cracked the original unit's AI commercial software and used it to start a new company for profit after leaving.
> 🔍 Law enforcement officers reconstructed the entire process of copying, cracking, and cross-company reuse through electronic data evidence collection, forming a complete chain of evidence.
> ⚖️ The involved party was fined 100,000 yuan and guided to improve confidentiality measures, marking the first confirmation that AIGC commercial software can be protected as commercial secrets.
