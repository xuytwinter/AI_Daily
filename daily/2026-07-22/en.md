8. OpenAI Officially Launches Advertising Service in ChatGPT, Best Buy Among the First to Test It

OpenAI introduced advertising services in ChatGPT, marking an important step in its commercialization process. Ads are targeted based on richer contextual signals, presented around the user's complete intent chain rather than just matching search terms. Currently, brands like Best Buy have participated in testing and expressed satisfaction with early results. OpenAI also designed several trust mechanisms to ensure the transparency of ads and the controllability of user data. This move transforms ChatGPT from a pure conversational tool into a "conversation as business" platform, potentially becoming a new variable in digital advertising.
