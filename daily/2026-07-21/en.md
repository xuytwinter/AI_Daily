5. XPeng unveils TuringViT visual encoder: Using only one-tenth of the data, yet surpassing the SOTA baseline

Xpeng Group's TuringViT high-efficiency visual encoder provides the industry with a low-cost, reproducible SOTA-level visual Transformer training path through systematic reconstruction of architecture, data paradigm, and training process. Its core advantage lies in data governance and training process optimization, achieving better results with only one-tenth of the data, promoting the popularization of advanced visual large models.

![image.png](https://pic.chinaz.com/picmap/202208080929359028_6.jpg)

> [AiBase Highlights:]
> 🚀 Architectural innovation: TuringViT adopts an architecture dominated by linear attention, improving efficiency and deployment capability at high resolutions.
> 🔍 Data governance: The VISTA-Curation pipeline enhances the value of each sample through multi-step screening, significantly improving data efficiency.
> 🔄 Training process optimization: A four-stage progressive native dynamic resolution training paradigm adapts to downstream task input characteristics, improving model performance.
> Details: https://turingvit.github.io/

6. YouTube tightens policies, cracking down on low-quality AI content

YouTube recently announced new policies, strictly cracking down on AI-generated low-quality content and prohibiting three types of non-real content from monetization, aiming to improve platform content quality and maintain ecological balance.

![image.png](https://pic.chinaz.com/picmap/201811151614001643_47.jpg)

> [AiBase Highlights:]
> 🚫 YouTube prohibits three types of non-real content from monetization, including repetitive templates, disturbing content, and AI virtual person videos.
> 🐾 Disturbing videos intentionally create pain and fear to increase click-through rates, and the platform will strictly crack down on such content.
> 📉 Channels that publish a large amount of violating content will lose the qualification to join the partner program and cannot monetize through ads.

8. Report: Alibaba to launch Qwen Office, integrating three intelligent agents to layout the AI office market
